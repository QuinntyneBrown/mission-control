Mission Control workflow mocks

Static HTML mocks of every page, dialog, notification, and pop-up described in the PRD and L2 requirements: 40 mocks with 356 states. Use the State menu in each mock's dark MOCK bar to switch between its states, or follow a state link below.

About these mocks

Nothing here is live

Every mock is labelled MOCK. Forms never submit, nothing is saved or sent, and simulated results only change what the page shows. Contact and work data is fictitious (example.org addresses, 555-01xx phone numbers, and the UK drama range +44 20 7946 0xxx for international formats); the only real identity is Quinntyne Brown, the City Lead.

Responsive by design

Each mock adapts from 320px to 1920px without page-level horizontal scrolling. Review at 375, 768, and 1440px (L2-048) and the profile R widths 320, 576, 992, 1200, and 1920px in the responsive viewer, or resize the browser. Design notes under the MOCK bar describe behaviour a picture can't show: focus, announcements, and rules.

Tokens and language

Visual values come from the --mc- tokens in assets/tokens.css. The standalone design system will adopt them as its authoritative copy. Mocks are design artifacts: they are reviewed manually and have no tests.
