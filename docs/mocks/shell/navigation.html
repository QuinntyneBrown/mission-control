At 992px and wider the sidebar shows labels. From 768–991px it becomes an icon rail; labels stay available to assistive technology. Below 768px a top bar with a menu button replaces it.

Collaborators see Home, Leads, and Projects only. Administration destinations are absent, not disabled; their direct URLs show the access-denied page.

View below 768px. Choosing a destination closes the menu and moves focus to the new page's heading. Escape or the close button returns focus to the menu button.

Sign out clears the in-memory session and opens the sign-in page with a "You've signed out" message. With unsaved changes, the unsaved-changes dialog appears first.

The skip link is the first focusable element. Every focusable control shows a 2px focus ring with a 2px offset; here it is shown on the Projects link. After each route change, focus moves to the page heading.
